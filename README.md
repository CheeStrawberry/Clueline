# Clueline

A plain-logic story check for mystery novelists. Clueline reads your manuscript, keeps the timeline, places and clues straight, and shows where a reveal comes too early or a clue sits where it shouldn't.

**Try it:** open the live page (GitHub Pages link) in a desktop browser.

- Demo login: `demo@clueline.app` / `demo1234`
- Six sample stories are included. Your own stories are saved in your browser only.
- Light, dark and "match my device" themes are in the account menu.

Built as a storytelling design competition entry.
